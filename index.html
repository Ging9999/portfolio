About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate, currently on a placement year as a Software Engineer Intern at Interact Software in Manchester.

At Interact I work on a production AI assistant: making its Python backend faster, hardening the pipeline that lets it execute tools safely, and tightening data access across the AI layer and the C#/ASP.NET API. Outside work I run LearnWell, an AI tutoring platform for CS students that runs entirely on local models.

I like working close to the metal and close to the risk: I wrote a 3D physics engine with my own maths library rather than pulling in GLM, and an encrypted, tamper-evident publishing app for journalists. I care about systems that are fast, correct and hard to misuse.
